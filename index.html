The vision

Two worlds.
One shared
future.

AI opens the possibility of a parallel world. A world of autonomous intelligence, connected to human reality and expanding what is possible within it.

A world beyond automation.

Today, AI enters everyday life one task at a time: writing, analysing, generating, automating. These are useful beginnings. Its greater potential may lie in what comes next.

Imagine a parallel world where intelligence can pursue a purpose, make decisions and carry work through to an outcome. A world where AI systems collaborate, build and operate autonomously.

To reach that future, the ambition has to grow: from automating tasks to constructing a world in which AI can act.

Reality, with a counterpart.

A counterpart carries something of its origin into another world. Connected to the same purpose, it can develop capabilities and act with agency of its own.

Imagine human knowledge and ambition taking on an independent life in the AI world. A living counterpart that learns, experiments and creates—and brings discoveries back into the physical world.

Money enables exchange in the human economy. Tokens offer an early metaphor for the currency of the AI world: units through which intelligence is accessed and consumed. The longer-term vision is an economy where autonomous systems can obtain resources, coordinate work and exchange value.

The connection starts here.

This parallel world has physical foundations. Intelligence needs compute. Compute needs energy. Autonomous systems need ways to interact with people, businesses and the world around them.

That is why AI and energy sit at the centre of Neo Genesis Ventures: investing in and building the foundations of this future, and the ventures that connect intelligence to real human needs.

The journey starts with those connections. Each venture is a chance to turn a part of this vision into something people can use.
